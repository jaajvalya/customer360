# Customer Intelligence AI — Executive Demo Script

**Presenter:** ACL Digital  
**Audience:** C-suite and senior business leaders (CEO, CFO, CMO, CIO, COO, Chief Customer Officer)  
**Suggested duration:** 18–22 minutes (plus 8–10 minutes for discussion)  
**Demo asset:** `kpi-dashboard.html` (Customer Intelligence Command Center)  
**Tone:** Professional, concise, outcome-oriented  

---

## Before you begin

- Open the dashboard in full screen; hide browser bookmarks and unrelated tabs.
- Start on **KPI Dashboard** (default landing screen).
- Have filters ready to change once (segment or region) to show the experience is scoped, not static.
- Set expectations in the room: *“This is a representative executive experience built on synthetic telecom data. The architecture, workflows, and AI patterns reflect what we deploy with live CRM, billing, network, and digital sources.”*

**Outcome headline to repeat:** One place to see subscriber health, act on churn and revenue, and govern AI recommendations—from leadership view to frontline action.

---

## 1. Opening — frame the business problem (2 minutes)

Good [morning/afternoon]. Thank you for your time.

Most communications organizations already invest in CRM, billing, care, network operations, and digital analytics. The gap is not data—it is **decision speed**. Leaders still reconcile spreadsheets; retention teams justify actions after the fact; marketing discovers segments months late; and digital friction shows up only when call volume spikes.

**Customer Intelligence AI** is designed to close that gap. It extends traditional Customer 360 with behavioral and predictive intelligence, and it prepares you for governed autonomous action—always with human approval where it matters.

What you will see today is not another dashboard. It is a **command layer** that answers three questions executives care about every week:

1. **Are we healthy?** Subscriber health, network experience, engagement, and revenue signals in one view.  
2. **Where is risk and upside?** Churn, friction, and AI-ranked opportunities with estimated impact.  
3. **What do we do next?** Recommendations you can approve, assign, or export to campaigns—not buried in a data lake.

Let’s walk through how that works in practice.

## 2. Platform story — four intelligence layers (1 minute)

*(Optional: point to the layer strip on the KPI Dashboard.)*

We organize capability in four layers—each builds on the last:

| Layer | What it delivers | Business outcome |
|-------|------------------|------------------|
| **1 — Traditional C360** | Who the subscriber is: products, lines, devices, account context | Single record of truth for care and sales |
| **2 — Behavior intelligence** | How they act: digital journeys, intent, friction | Fix experiences before they drive churn or cost |
| **3 — Predictive intelligence** | What they will likely do: churn, upsell, hidden segments | Prioritize retention and growth spend |
| **4 — Autonomous intelligence** | Act in real time—with copilot and agents under your policies | Scale execution without losing control |

Today’s demo focuses on layers 1–3 in depth, with a preview of layer 4 on the roadmap.

## Appendix B — Likely executive questions (short answers)

**How is this different from our BI tool?**  
BI reports what happened. This prioritizes what to do next, with AI-ranked risk and revenue and workflows to approve actions.

**Do we need to replace our CRM?**  
No. We integrate with CRM, billing, care, network, and digital analytics. Customer 360+ aggregates; it does not replace systems of record.

**How do you handle AI trust and compliance?**  
Explainability on churn, human approval on recommendations, model versioning, and scoped data by region/segment—aligned to telecom regulatory expectations.

**Timeline to production?**  
Pilot in roughly 90 days with defined integrations; scale depends on source complexity and governance requirements.

**Who owns this internally?**  
Often a joint **customer experience / marketing / IT** steering group, with clear executive sponsorship from revenue or customer operations.
